# Email Assistant privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-14.

Email Assistant is operated by Jason Doran for mailboxes he owns or administers. The Google authorization screen currently uses the name **Codex Job Search Gmail Connector**. This policy describes Email Assistant’s monitoring and related email-assistance workflow.

## Information accessed and its purpose

With authorization, the Gmail read-only connection accesses message identifiers, conversation identifiers, sender and recipient details, subjects, timestamps, labels, and message text. Where Gmail stores message text as a MIME attachment, the reader retrieves that text. The monitor does not generally download file attachments.

This information supports reply detection, contact recognition from prior Sent messages, job-search and sales monitoring, follow-up reminders, duplicate prevention, and coverage checks. A clearly matched job-application rejection can update the corresponding private opportunity record without generating a rejection alert.

## Processing and sharing

Email information may be provided to OpenAI’s Codex or ChatGPT services to assist with classification, conversation review, and requested drafting. This workflow is not exclusively local processing. Information handled by these services is also subject to the applicable account settings and provider terms.

Approved Telegram destinations receive selected information such as sender details, subjects, message links, signal categories, and follow-up reminders. Group members can see information posted to their destination. The system shares this information to provide the email-assistance features requested by Jason.

Interactive drafting may store message text in private ChatGPT Pages and use the separately authorized Gmail plugin. The direct monitoring connection itself has no permission to send or modify email.

## Storage and retention

Monitoring state and private records are stored on Jason’s computer. Records can include message metadata, classification results, contact and follow-up state, delivery receipts, and selected draft or Sent examples. Selected correspondence may be retained to preserve conversation context and improve Jason’s personal writing guidance. Private backups or archives may also contain these records.

Temporary email captures may be used during processing. Some information can remain in Codex conversations, Pages, Telegram messages, or other provider records under their respective retention controls. There is no automatic fixed deletion period for all records; revoking Google access does not delete information already retained.

The dedicated Google refresh credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Credentials and private mailbox records are excluded from the public documentation repository.

## Choices and deletion requests

Mailbox owners can revoke access through [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revocation stops future access through that authorization. To request removal of retained local records or assistance removing copies in connected services, contact Jason Doran at [jason.p.doran@gmail.com](mailto:jason.p.doran@gmail.com). Copies in backups or third-party services may require separate handling.

## Changes

Material changes to data use will be described here before they take effect. Additional Google permissions require separate authorization.
